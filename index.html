Out of Sorts: Ball Sort Puzzle ("the app") is developed by Weast Coast Games. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Data we collect

None directly. The app needs no account. Your progress, stars, boosters, streak and settings are saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not receive your personal information.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). The app shows optional rewarded ads only when you tap a button marked with an ad icon. To serve and measure these ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad-interaction data, and may show personalized ads where you have consented. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland (and where US state law requires it), Google's consent form asks for your choices before any ad is requested. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and AdMob's data disclosure.
  • Google Play Billing. If you buy Premium or the Starter Pack, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We receive only a purchase confirmation needed to unlock what you bought; we never see your payment details.

Your choices

  • You never have to watch an ad: ads play only when you choose a button marked with the ad icon.
  • You can review or change your ad consent at any time from Settings → Privacy & consent in the app (shown where consent applies).
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all game data stored on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and transfers

Data processed by Google is encrypted in transit and handled under Google's security standards, and may be processed on servers outside your country.

Changes

If our practices change, we will update this page and its effective date.
